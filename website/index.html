Smart nutrition tracking, stripped of clutter

Everything you need to hit your health goals without the bloat of traditional calorie tracking apps.

✨

AI Natural Language Logging

Type your meal or workout in plain English (like "grilled chicken breast with quinoa and avocado"). Calcifer automatically calculates carbs, fat, protein, and calories.

🎯

Intuitive Macro & Calorie Budgets

Visual radial charts and progress bars display your remaining daily calorie targets and carbohydrate, fat, and protein thresholds at a single glance.

🔒

Privacy-First & Offline

Your data is yours alone. No accounts, no emails, no remote tracking. Everything is saved locally on your device with lightning-fast offline access.

⚡

One-Tap Quick Logging

Quickly re-add frequent meals and activities from your recent library. Adjust portion sizes in grams or duration in minutes seamlessly.

📊

Visual Analytics & Stats

Monitor 7-day, 30-day, 90-day, and all-time macro balance trends with interactive multi-metric charts and daily average bars.

⚙️

Customizable Goals & Splits

Fine-tune your personal Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR), TDEE, weight goals, and customize your exact percentage split for carbs, fat, and protein.
